About Me

Who Am I?

I’m Nia Clark from Atlanta, GA—a self-identified Blerd who has always had a passion for coding and creativity. My journey into tech began with UX/UI design before enrolling at App Academy, where I transformed my accessible designs into functional sites. In just a short time, I went from knowing a bit of HTML and CSS about coding to launching three live websites—a huge accomplishment!

Growing up with an interest in technology sparked my desire to learn coding at a young age. I remember tinkering with computers and dreaming of creating my own applications someday. That dream is now becoming a reality as I’ve gained proficiency in languages like HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, SQL, Python—and frameworks like React and Redux. Each line of code feels like building blocks toward something greater.

My previous work experiences have taught me the importance of teamwork and communication—skills that are invaluable in tech. Whether it was managing fast-paced customer interactions at Dominos or organizing shipments at PVH Corp., I've learned how to adapt quickly while maintaining a positive attitude. These experiences have shaped me into someone who thrives under pressure and values collaboration.

Outside of work and coding bootcamps, I find joy in anime—a passion that fuels my creativity and inspires my designs. It’s fascinating how storytelling through art can translate into user experience design! This blend of interests keeps me motivated as I continue to grow in this field.
